Who runs this site

This site is run by HonestBaking, a shop on Etsy that sells spreadsheet tools for home bakers. To get in touch, send a message through the HonestBaking shop on Etsy.

Terms of use

The tools on this site are free to use. They give estimates from the numbers you type in. They are not financial, tax or legal advice, and they do not know your costs, your local rules or your market. You decide your own prices and use the results at your own risk.

The serving counts come from the Wilton serving guide, as stated on the chart page. Your own cakes may cut differently.

The pages were built with AI assistance.

Privacy policy

These pages use no cookies and no analytics, and they have no forms that send anything. The numbers you type stay in your browser: the pages do not store them and do not send them anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the IP address of each visitor and keeps its own logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Links to Etsy on this site are the shop's own Share & Save links. When you follow one you leave this site, and Etsy's own terms and privacy policy apply there.
