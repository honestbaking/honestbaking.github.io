Cake serving chart and price list

How many slices each cake size gives, in party and wedding cuts, and what each size costs at your price per serving.

ShapeSizeServingsPrice

Serving counts follow the Wilton cake serving guide for cakes about 4 inches tall (two layers). Wilton does not list an 18 x 24 inch sheet; it is counted here as two 12 x 18 half sheets. Cakes under 3 inches tall serve about half as many. Tiered cakes are round tiers added together.

Where does the price per serving come from?

A price per serving only works if it covers your ingredients, packaging, time, overhead and card fees. The Cake Pricing Calculator spreadsheet (Excel and Google Sheets) works that out for each cake from your own ingredient prices, then fills this same size and tier price list with your number, and prints a customer quote.
